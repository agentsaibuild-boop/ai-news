## 🏛️ Policy, Regulation & Governance

- **Meta's landmark child-safety trial opened Tuesday in Oakland federal court**, with attorneys general from California, Colorado, Kentucky and New Jersey (of 29 suing states) alleging Facebook and Instagram were built to addict children, concealed harms, and illegally collected minors' data. Meta says theoretical exposure runs as high as **$1.4 trillion**; the states also want addictive design features — infinite scroll, visible likes, beauty filters — removed for under-18s. Zuckerberg and Instagram chief Adam Mosseri are expected to testify. ## 🧠 AI Fun Fact of the Day

> In February 2011, IBM's Watson famously crushed Jeopardy! champions Ken Jennings and Brad Rutter — but in Final Jeopardy, under the category "U.S. Cities," it answered "What is Toronto?????" The five question marks signaled its own low confidence, and it had hedged its bet to just $947, so it still won the match comfortably — a neat early lesson in making AI systems that know when they don't know.
