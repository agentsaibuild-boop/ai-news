## 🧠 AI Fun Fact of the Day

> The word "robot" wasn't coined by an engineer but by a painter: when Czech writer Karel Čapek needed a name for the artificial workers in his 1920 play *R.U.R.*, he planned to call them "laboři" — until his brother Josef, a cubist painter, suggested "roboti," from *robota*, the old Czech word for forced labor. The play was a global hit, and within a decade "robot" had entered virtually every language on Earth.
