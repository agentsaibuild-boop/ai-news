## ⭐ Top Story

**Ads are coming to ChatGPT in Europe: OpenAI announced Tuesday that on August 24 it will start serving ads across 31 European markets — its largest ads expansion to date.** Ads will appear only for **Free** and **Go (€8/month)** users; Plus, Pro and Enterprise stay ad-free. OpenAI says ads are "always clearly labeled and separate from ChatGPT's answers," that answers are never influenced by advertisers, and that conversations aren't shared or sold — the six-month delay versus the U.S. rollout came from building GDPR-compliant consent flows for personalized targeting. Advertisers start via OpenAI's Ads Solutions team and partners, with self-service Ads Manager to follow this summer. ## 🚀 Model Releases & Capability

- **Z.ai's GLM-5.3 is rolling out as the self-proclaimed "strongest open-weights coding model."** Released last Friday (Aug 14) via API and the GLM Coding Plan and landing on gateways this week, it reuses GLM-5.2's **743B-parameter MoE** base — every gain comes from scaled-up post-training alone, which Z.ai says delivered a ~50% improvement over its predecessor on coding tasks. Trained on vulnerability-hunting environments, the model helped find **2,436 vulnerabilities across 269 projects** (some decades old); downloadable weights follow in about two weeks after a security review. It works with ZCode, Claude Code and OpenCode. ## 🧠 AI Fun Fact of the Day

> Stanford's MYCIN (early 1970s) — an expert system for diagnosing blood infections — beat human doctors in a blinded evaluation published in JAMA in 1979: its antibiotic recommendations were rated acceptable 65% of the time, versus 42.5–62.5% for Stanford's own infectious-disease faculty. Yet MYCIN was never used on a single real patient — unresolved questions about legal liability and the sheer awkwardness of 30-minute terminal sessions kept the best diagnostician in the building switched off.
