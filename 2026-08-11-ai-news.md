## ⭐ Top Story

**Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter open-weights model under Apache 2.0 — its most permissively licensed frontier-lab release yet, built to run local AI agents on a single consumer GPU.** Distilled from the flagship Muse Spark via logit distillation, Glimmer is tuned for local agent workflows, function calling, coding and LLM-as-a-judge evaluation, trained on data from 100+ languages; 4-bit quantization compresses it from 55+ GB to under 20 GB so it fits a 24 GB consumer card with ~1% quality degradation. Meta is shipping full BF16 weights, 4-bit variants, the DFlash drafter and the perception encoder on Hugging Face, with support rolling out through Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, llama.cpp, MLX and major API hosts. [Meta AI Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-glimmer-open-agentic-model) · [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meta-returns-to-open-source-with-muse-glimmer-an-apache-2-0-licensed-30b-parameter-ai-model-optimized-for-agents-available-now)

## 🚀 Model Releases & Capability

- **OpenAI launched GPT-5.6-Cyber — its first model shipped at the "High" cyber threshold — and split Daybreak into Blue and Red tiers.** Announced Monday, the purpose-trained model completes **95%** of advanced offensive-security tasks (exploit chains, authentication bypass, privilege escalation) versus 57.3% for GPT-5.5-Cyber and ~1.5% for standard GPT-5.6 Sol. In testing it found two previously unknown Chrome V8 flaws chainable to escape the heap sandbox (Google assigned CVE-2026-15903) plus at least five bugs in a popular mobile OS. Access is gated: **Daybreak Blue** gives vetted defenders frontier models for incident response and malware analysis; **Daybreak Red** restricts GPT-5.6-Cyber to trusted partners like Accenture, IBM, CrowdStrike and Cloudflare, with hardware security keys mandatory for all Daybreak accounts from **September 1**. It lands days after OpenAI paused its Astra frontier model over potentially "Critical" cyber capabilities.
