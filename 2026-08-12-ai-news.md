## ⭐ Top Story

**Nvidia signed memorandums of understanding with six Wall Street giants — Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR — to build a $500 billion financing platform for AI infrastructure.** The plan turns compute into a lendable asset class: special-purpose vehicles will issue private offerings and bonds collateralized by compute power, with individual vehicles able to raise tens of billions and Nvidia itself providing up to 25% of the financing per deal. ## 🚀 Model Releases & Capability

- **Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning, a free-for-commercial-use open model built for high-volume AI agents.** The 30B-parameter mixture-of-experts model activates just **3B parameters per token**, so it runs on a single consumer RTX GPU or DGX Spark; Nvidia claims up to **4× faster output** and ~30% quicker agentic task completion than comparable open models. Distilled from Nemotron 3 Ultra, it ships on Hugging Face, ModelScope and OpenRouter alongside **NeMo Switchyard**, an open-source router that sends each agent request to the most efficient model. A day after Meta's Muse Glimmer, the "frontier agents on one GPU" race is officially crowded. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-releases-nemotron-3point5-lightning-open-source-ai-model-.html) · [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-lightning-switchyard-rtx-dgx/)

## 🧠 AI Fun Fact of the Day

> Shakey the Robot (SRI, 1966–1972) — the first mobile robot that could reason about its own actions — moved so slowly and shook so much that it earned its name from its own wobble. The A* pathfinding algorithm was invented specifically to get Shakey across a room, and it still routes characters in nearly every video game today.
