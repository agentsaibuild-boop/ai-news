## ⭐ Top Story

**Kimi K3's open weights are live — the largest open-weight release in AI history.** Moonshot AI published the full weights of its 2.8-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model (~50B active parameters per token, 1-million-token context) at 00:00 UTC today, roughly 75% larger than the previous record holder, DeepSeek's V4-Pro. The catch is physics: even in 4-bit MXFP4 precision the weights occupy ~1.4 terabytes, needing around eighteen 80-GB accelerators just to load — so "open" in practice serves clouds and large enterprises on Nvidia Blackwell or AMD MI400 silicon, not workstations. Third-party evaluations place K3 just behind Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol Max on intelligence indexes, and first in frontend-coding tasks. Self-hosting also removes the China data-sovereignty question that routing through Moonshot's API raises. ## 🚀 Model Releases & Capability

- **Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5 on Friday — near-Fable-5 capability at half the price.** The new model tops FrontierBench v0.1 with 43.3% versus GPT-5.6 Sol's 37.5% (Fable 5 scores 33.7%), is described as "much stronger at verifying its work and iterating carefully until it succeeds," and adds a low/medium/high effort toggle. API pricing stays at $5 input / $25 output per million tokens (unchanged from Opus 4.8), it is now the default on Claude Max, safeguards are expected to trigger ~85% less often than on Fable 5, and — unlike Fable/Mythos — it carries no 30-day data-retention policy. It's Anthropic's fourth flagship in under two months, after Mythos 5, Fable 5 and Sonnet 5. ## 🏛️ Policy, Regulation & Governance

- **The White House's voluntary frontier-model framework is due by Friday, August 1.** Stemming from the June 2 executive order, it gives federal agencies a 30-day pre-release review window on covered frontier models, with OpenAI, Anthropic and Google finalizing participation. Today's Kimi K3 drop underlines the framework's blind spot: it is built around closed-API models, and open-weight releases can't be recalled or gated after download. - **One week to the EU AI Act's August 2 date — and it's transparency, not high-risk, that comes due.** The Digital Omnibus (final approval June 29) deferred the heavy high-risk obligations to December 2, 2027 (Annex III) and August 2, 2028 (Annex I products), but Article 50 stays on schedule: from next Sunday, users must be told when they interact with an AI system and AI-generated content must be disclosed. One narrow reprieve: the machine-readable marking requirement is deferred to December 2, 2026 for systems already deployed before August 2. ## 🧠 AI Fun Fact of the Day

> On January 7, 1954, the Georgetown–IBM experiment had an IBM 701 translate more than sixty Russian sentences into English — using just six grammar rules and a 250-word vocabulary — and the researchers confidently predicted machine translation would be a solved problem within three to five years. The hype unlocked a decade of government funding before the 1966 ALPAC report concluded progress had stalled, freezing US machine-translation research for years — an early rehearsal of the AI hype cycle.
